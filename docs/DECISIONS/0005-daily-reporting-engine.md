# ADR 0003 — Daily Reporting Engine as Core Intelligence Layer

## Status

Accepted

---

# Context

Most construction systems treat daily reports as administrative paperwork.

This creates poor operational visibility and unreliable analytics.

BetavanX requires a reliable operational intelligence foundation.

---

# Decision

BetavanX will treat daily reporting as the primary operational intelligence layer.

Daily reports are not considered simple CRUD records.

They are considered:

Operational Reality Capture.

All future intelligence systems will derive from this operational data pipeline.

---

# Implications

The Daily Reporting Engine becomes a critical strategic system.

Future architecture must support:

- high-quality operational data
- structured reporting
- workforce tracking
- productivity analysis
- delay analysis
- trend intelligence
- AI-ready datasets

---

# Strategic Principle

No intelligence system can exceed the quality of its operational reporting layer.

Therefore:

The Daily Reporting Engine is the heart of BetavanX.
