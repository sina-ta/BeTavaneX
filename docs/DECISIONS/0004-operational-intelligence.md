# ADR 0002 — Operational Intelligence Foundation

## Status

Accepted

---

# Context

The project evolved beyond a simple CRUD construction dashboard.

The system required:

- operational analytics
- KPI intelligence
- recommendation infrastructure
- workforce scoring
- historical trend tracking

However,
introducing AI or realtime systems too early
would destabilize the architecture.

---

# Decision

Create Operational Intelligence Foundation BEFORE:

- AI systems
- websocket systems
- predictive analytics
- advanced automation

Focus areas:

- KPI tracking
- trend architecture
- worker intelligence
- explainable recommendations
- analytics foundation

---

# Consequences

Positive:

- stable intelligence foundation
- explainable operational logic
- scalable analytics architecture

Negative:

- delayed advanced AI systems
- delayed realtime features

---

# Strategic Principle

BetavanX should evolve from:

MVP Dashboard

→ Enterprise Architecture

→ Operational Intelligence Platform

→ AI-Assisted Construction Intelligence System

in controlled architectural phases.
